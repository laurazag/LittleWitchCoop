CUSTOM EMOTE SOUNDS
Place WAV files in the game folder under:
BepInEx\config\LittleWitchCoop\EmoteSounds

carinho.wav   - heart
risada.wav    - laugh
oi.wav        - wave
palminhas.wav - clap
surpresa.wav  - surprise
soninho.wav   - sleepy
flor.wav      - flower
magia.wav     - magic

Use 16-bit PCM WAV, mono/stereo, 8-96 kHz, up to 5 seconds and 2 MB per file.
For short files, mono at 22050 Hz is sufficient. Export/convert MP3 to PCM WAV;
renaming an extension does not convert the audio.
Files are reloaded each time an emote plays. Missing/invalid files use the
built-in chime. Share the same WAV files if everyone should hear the same sounds;
the mod sends emote identifiers, not audio files. Custom sound volume follows
the emote source level; adjust individual audio files to balance their loudness.
